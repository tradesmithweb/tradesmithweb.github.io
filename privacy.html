Privacy

This notice covers tradesmithweb.com and the free previews we make. Our postal address is in our terms.

What we collect

From this site's form or your email: your business name, city, email address and, if you choose, a phone number.

To build a free preview before you contact us, we may also use public business details we find online: the business name, address, phone number, services and public reviews. We use these only to make your draft and to email you about it.

What we use it for

To build your free website preview, reply to you and, if you become a client, run your site and invoice you. Nothing else.

Previews

Preview pages are hidden from search engines and shared only with the business they were made for. If you did not ask for one, you can ask us to take it down.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We don't sell or rent your details. Form messages pass through Web3Forms, the service that delivers them to our inbox. Email runs through Zoho Mail. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

Removing your details

Email us and we delete your details, and any preview made for you, within 30 days. If you also want no more emails from us, say so and we stop.
