Before we get there

Water heater leaking? Do this first.

Many Austin homes have the water heater in the attic, so a slow leak can reach the ceiling fast. These steps stop most of the damage.

Smell gas? Leave the house first, then call your gas company from outside.

  1. Shut off the cold waterTurn the valve on the cold pipe at the top of the tank until it stops.
  2. Turn off the heatGas: turn the control knob to OFF. Electric: switch off its breaker.
  3. Check the panIn the attic, see if the drain pan is overflowing and put towels under any wet spot.
  4. Call usWe'll tell you on the phone whether it can wait until morning.
