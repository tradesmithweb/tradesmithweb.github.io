Insurance claims

Your hail claim in four steps

Most of our roofs are paid for by insurance. Here is how it goes, start to finish.

Texas law (HB 2102) makes it illegal for a roofer to pay or waive your deductible. We never offer it, and you should be wary of anyone who does.

  1. Free inspectionWe go up, photograph every slope and give you a written report.
  2. File the claimIf there's real damage, you call your insurer. We give you photos and a report to send them.
  3. Meet the adjusterWe meet them on the roof and point out every hit.
  4. New roofOnce approved, we install it and send the paperwork your insurer needs.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. No charge and no pressure, whether or not you file a claim.

Will my insurance pay for a new roof?

If hail or wind damaged it, often yes, minus your deductible. We'll tell you honestly if the damage isn't enough for a claim.

How long does a replacement take?

Most homes take one or two days.

Do you do metal roofs?

Yes, standing seam and exposed-fastener panels for homes, barns and shops.

Can you cover my deductible?

No. Texas law doesn't allow it, and any roofer who offers is breaking the law.
