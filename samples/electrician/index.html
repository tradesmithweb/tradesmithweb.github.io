FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Not answered here?Call (713) 555-0118
Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. We do a load check first, and many homes can add a charger without a new panel.

How big a generator do I need?

It depends on what you want running. Essentials only is smaller and cheaper than the whole house. We size it on a visit.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Every electrician is licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, whenever your city requires one, like panel swaps and generators.

Is a tripping breaker dangerous?

It's doing its job, but it means something is wrong. If you smell burning or see scorch marks, call right away.
